Front Desk widget
DemoFor cafes

One line on your site.

The chat button in the corner of this page is the real widget, running on the demo bakery's content. Open it and ask about hours, cakes or delivery.

Install

Paste this before </body>. The content file holds your business details and pages; I prepare it from your site.

<script src="https://phazww.github.io/front-desk/widget.js"
        data-content="https://your-site.com/front-desk.json"
        data-color="#b4532a" async></script>

Two modes

Quote mode (this page)

No model at all. It finds the part of your site that matches the question and shows it word for word, or gives your phone number. Nothing can be made up and nothing is paid per question.

Answer mode

With a model endpoint added, it writes a short answer, and the same checks as in the demo run before it is shown: quote on the site, every number matching, every word backed by the site. If a check fails, it falls back to quote mode.
